bébé tracker

A shared newborn logbook. Feeds, diapers, pumping, mom's meds, weight and appointments, in sync across your phones.

Quick start

  1. Open bebe.fyi and tap "Start your household".One per baby. No sign-up.
  2. Answer four quick questions.Baby's name and birth time, whose phone this is, how often feeds come around. All changeable later.
  3. Send the link to your partner.They open it, tap "I have a family link", and they're in the same log. The link is the key, so keep it between you.
  4. Add it to both home screens.iPhone: Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu, Add to Home screen.
  5. Log the first feed.Tap Feed, tap a volume, tap Save. The countdown starts.

The five screens

Today. Countdown to the next feed, asleep or awake and for how long, the last pump, mom's meds as countdowns or last-taken times, today's totals, the latest weight, the next appointment. The dock logs anything in one to three taps.

Summary. One row per day, the numbers the pediatrician asks for. Tap a day for its entries, or switch to All entries to filter, edit and delete.

Weight. Birth weight first, then each weigh-in in pounds and ounces. Every entry shows the change from birth and from last time.

Appts. Doctor or place, what it's for, when. The next one shows on Today.

Settings. Feed and med intervals from the discharge sheet, the Tylenol and Ibuprofen rotation, your names, mL or oz, and the family link.

Logging in the dark

Every sheet opens at now.Tap −5 or +5 to nudge, or tap the time for a full picker.
Wet is two taps.Diaper, then Wet, saved. Dirty asks for a colour, and the colour tap saves.
Made a mistake?Tap an entry to edit. Swipe left to delete. Undo sits at the bottom for ten seconds.
Mom's meds count down too.Enter the hours between doses in Settings. The app never suggests an interval or a dose.
Sleep is one button.Tap Sleep when she drifts off: where (contact, bassinet, stroller) and any help (held, carrier, pacifier). Moved her? Tap her sleep card. While she sleeps the button reads Woke up; one tap ends it.
Longer stretches at night?Settings → Feed timer → Different at night. A feed that starts after night begins counts down the night hours.
No signal, no problem.Entries save on the phone and sync when you're back online.

Good to know

The link is the key. No accounts or passwords. Anyone with your household link can read and add to your log, so only share it with your partner.

Opened it on a new phone and the log is empty? Tap "I have a family link" on the welcome screen, or "Wrong household on this device?" in Settings, and paste your link.

Where's the data? On Google's Firebase under your household's random address. Settings has a CSV export, a full JSON backup, and a Delete button for when you're done.
